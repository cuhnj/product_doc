## 公司介绍 / Company Introduction

### 中文

**南京创优科技有限责任公司**，自 2003 年创始之初就致力于智能振动送料控制系统的自主研发、生产和销售。

作为中国振动送料控制领域的头部企业和行业标准制定者，产品线涵盖全系列电磁、压电、音圈电机振动送料控制器，以及传送带、离心盘、提升机等控制器。

创优科技累计投入过亿研发资金，坚持创新驱动，自主研发自动稳速技术、无振动传感器稳速技术、全面保护技术等，助力中国新质生产力发展。

**企业荣誉（节选）：** 国家级高新技术企业；国家级科技中小型企业；国家级专精特新「小巨人」企业；江苏省智能振动控制工程技术研究中心；中国机械工业联合会智能制造分会会员单位等。

**联系方式：** 客服 400-985-8889 · 地址：南京市江宁区智能路 9 号启迪城学研园 2 幢

以上内容摘自公司产品手册 V8.01「企业介绍 / 技术引领 / 企业荣誉」。完整手册见下方「产品手册」。

### English

**Nanjing CUH Science & Technology Co., Ltd.** has focused on the R&D, manufacturing, and sales of intelligent vibratory feeder control systems since its founding in 2003.

As a leading enterprise and industry standard contributor in vibratory feeder control in China, CUH offers a full lineup of electromagnetic, piezoelectric, and voice-coil motor feeder controllers, as well as controllers for conveyors, centrifugal bowls, elevators, and related equipment.

CUH continues to invest heavily in R&D, with proprietary technologies such as automatic speed stabilization, sensorless speed control, and comprehensive protection, supporting advanced manufacturing applications worldwide.

**Selected credentials:** National High-Tech Enterprise; National-level Specialized & Sophisticated “Little Giant” enterprise; Jiangsu Intelligent Vibration Control Engineering Technology Research Center; member of the Intelligent Manufacturing Branch of the China Machinery Industry Federation; and others.

**Contact:** Tel. 400-985-8889 · Headquarters: Building 2, Xueyan Tech Park, TusCity, No. 9 Zhineng Rd, Jiangning, Nanjing, China
